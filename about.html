About the Workshop

Last Lantern Workshop™ is an independent home for apps, games and digital creations. We build the things we're curious about, one project at a time.

Different ideas belong under the same roof: a post-apocalyptic world to explore, a practical way to organize a precious-metals collection, and an oracle experience shaped by mystery and imagination.

There's no single kind of project here. Just a workshop, a few tools, and another light burning for whatever comes next.
