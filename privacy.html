# MetalKeep Privacy Policy **Effective:** October 19, 2026 MetalKeep is a precious-metals collection and portfolio tracking application published by Last Lantern Workshop™. We respect your privacy. MetalKeep does not require you to create an account with Last Lantern Workshop to track your collection. ## Information you enter MetalKeep stores the holdings, purchase information, notes, and other collection details you enter on your device. Last Lantern Workshop does not operate a server that collects or stores your personal collection records. Your information may leave your device when you choose to export, share, or back up files, or when Android's optional device backup functionality is enabled. ## Photos and files MetalKeep allows you to attach photographs to your holdings and import or export collection information. The app may request access necessary to select, read, create, or save files. Photographs and collection files are not uploaded to Last Lantern Workshop servers. If you share or export files, their subsequent storage and handling depend on the service or location you select. ## Advertising The free version of MetalKeep displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect or process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP addresses, approximate location derived from network information, and advertising interaction data. This information may be used for advertising delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and personalization where permitted. Purchasing MetalKeep Premium removes advertisements from the app. For information about Google's data practices, visit Google's Privacy Policy. ## Advertising consent and privacy choices Where required by applicable law, MetalKeep provides advertising consent notices and privacy choices through Google's consent-management services. Depending on your location and applicable requirements, you may be able to manage choices concerning personalized advertising and related data processing. When MetalKeep's Offline Mode is enabled, the app does not request advertisements. ## Online services and metal prices When online price updates are enabled, MetalKeep connects to external services to retrieve metal pricing information. These requests may involve technical information ordinarily transmitted during internet communications, such as IP addresses and request details. Last Lantern Workshop does not intentionally include your personal collection records in metal-price requests. ## Google Play purchases MetalKeep Premium is offered as a one-time purchase through Google Play. Google processes payments. Last Lantern Workshop does not receive or store your full payment-card details. MetalKeep may receive purchase and entitlement information necessary to activate and restore Premium features. ## PIN and biometrics MetalKeep offers optional PIN and biometric security features. Biometric authentication uses Android's security functionality. MetalKeep does not receive or store your fingerprint or biometric template. The app may store security-related settings and authentication information necessary to operate its optional PIN protection. ## Backup and restore MetalKeep provides manual backup and restore features so you can maintain copies of your collection information. Android may also back up eligible app data through its device-managed backup services, depending on your device settings, Android version, and backup availability. Android-managed backups are separate from MetalKeep's manual backup system and may be associated with your Google account. Automatic backups are subject to Android's storage limits and may not include every photograph or record. ## Data retention and deletion Collection information remains stored on your device until you edit, delete, or remove it, subject to copies retained in exported files or backups. You can delete holdings within MetalKeep. Uninstalling the app generally removes its locally stored app data, but previously created backups or exported files may remain. Google and other third-party services manage information they process according to their own policies. ## Children's privacy MetalKeep is intended for general audiences and is not specifically directed toward children under 13. Last Lantern Workshop does not knowingly collect children's personal collection information through its own servers. ## Changes to this policy We may update this policy when MetalKeep's features, services, or privacy practices change. The current version will be available on the Last Lantern Workshop website, with an updated effective date when changes are made. ## Contact For questions about MetalKeep or this Privacy Policy, contact Last Lantern Workshop™ through the support contact information provided on our website. --- © 2026 Last Lantern Workshop™
