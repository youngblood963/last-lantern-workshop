MetalKeep Privacy Policy

Effective: September 24, 2026

MetalKeep is a precious-metals collection and portfolio tracking application published by Last Lantern Workshop™. This policy explains how information is handled when you use MetalKeep.

Information you enter

MetalKeep lets you enter information about your physical-metal holdings, including metal type, quantity, weight, purchase information, notes, and images you choose to attach. Collection information is designed to remain on your device unless you deliberately use an export, backup, restore, or other feature that moves or copies it.

Photos and files

If you add photos or import/export data, MetalKeep may request access necessary for you to select, create, read, or save those files. These permissions are used to provide the feature you request.

Internet and price information

When online features are enabled, MetalKeep may connect to internet services to obtain information such as precious-metal spot prices. Technical information ordinarily required to make an internet request, such as an IP address, may be processed by the receiving service.

Google Play purchases

Premium purchases are processed through Google Play Billing. Last Lantern Workshop™ does not receive or store your full payment-card information. MetalKeep may receive purchase-status information needed to unlock or restore premium features.

Security

MetalKeep may offer privacy, PIN, or biometric-related features. Biometric authentication is handled through supported Android system functionality; MetalKeep does not receive your fingerprint or biometric template.

Backups and exports

If you create a backup or export, you control where that file is stored or shared. Store such files securely.

Advertising

MetalKeep may offer an ad-supported free experience. If advertising is enabled in a distributed version, applicable data handling will be disclosed in MetalKeep's Google Play Data safety information and this policy will be updated as appropriate.

Children

MetalKeep is not designed specifically for children.

Financial disclaimer

MetalKeep is a collection and portfolio tracking tool. It is not a bank, broker, exchange, investment adviser, or financial institution. Price information may be delayed or inaccurate and should not be relied upon as financial or investment advice.

Changes

We may update this policy as MetalKeep changes. The effective date above will be updated when appropriate.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, use the developer contact information provided on MetalKeep's Google Play listing.
