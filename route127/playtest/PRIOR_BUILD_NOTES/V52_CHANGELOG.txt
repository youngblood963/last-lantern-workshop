ROUTE 127 v52 — CRIME & WANDERER INVENTORY PLAYTEST CANDIDATE

NEW
- Wanderers receive persistent, occupation-influenced stock and carried Bolts.
- STEAL command for wanderers in town or on roads. One attempt per encounter; repeat encounters permit new attempts.
- Stolen stock removed from trade inventory; remaining supplies/Bolts may be salvaged after a defeat.
- Failed theft generates warnings/suspicion; detected town theft raises bounty. Road theft does not.
- Unprovoked Solace wanderer attacks and kills generate distinct criminal records.
- Active bounty, lifetime bounty and recorded crimes; WANTED status in red, including early levels.
- WANTED POSTERS / WANTED commands on Solace board; PAY FINE at Tim clears active bounty, retains history.
- Level 5 bounty hunting unlock noted on wanted board. NPC contracts NOT implemented in this patch.
- No vendor or named story NPC theft, no multiplayer/player chest burglary.

QA / KNOWN LIMITATIONS
- Existing legacy and expansion automated suites pass. These suites do not exercise new v52 theft/bounty gameplay.
- New v52 systems are NOT browser verified. Please test save/load, repeated encounters, town crime, road theft,
  Tim fines, and trading inventory synchronization manually before treating as stable.
- Wanderer carried stock is generated once per persistent named wanderer; it does not refresh daily.
- The existing fixed combat Bolt reward remains in addition to carried Bolt salvage.
- No actual bounty hunting contracts yet; level-5 unlock is display-only.
- This is a targeted foundation, not the entire planned criminal justice / multiplayer system.
