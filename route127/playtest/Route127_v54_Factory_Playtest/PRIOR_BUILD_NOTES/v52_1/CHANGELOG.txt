CONFIRMED FIXES
1. Wanderer stock quantities and carried inventory now share the same item records. First purchases no longer double-decrement carried stock. Theft and salvage each remove one item exactly once.
2. Purchases transfer the player's payment to the wanderer's carried Bolts. Confirmed player sales transfer one exact carried item to the victim and deduct that wanderer's payment. A wanderer without enough carried Bolts cannot mint a sale payment. Existing item prices are unchanged.
3. Saved stock/inventory references are relinked before transactions. Old v52 mismatched quantities retain the lower remaining count; no previously removed goods are resurrected. Already-lost items from the old double-decrement cannot be reconstructed. v51 saves lacking crime inventory fields seed lazily from their saved stock.
4. Assault is recorded only after an actual town fight begins, not when a zero-turn confirmation is rejected.
5. Murder uses the current defeated combat's source and canonical victim identity. Uppercase combat names work. Fleeing/old victim flags cannot charge a subsequent road kill. Save/load retains combat location without recharging assault.
6. Crime records include the specific Solace establishment/location. Failed theft is witnessed by the caught victim; successful unseen theft and road crimes retain their existing no-bounty behavior.
7. Trade context no longer traps INSPECT, SMALL TALK or ATTACK/FIGHT. Town trade/theft correctly focuses the actual wanderer. Stale trade/focus cannot remotely buy/steal from an absent victim. A named road theft cannot silently target a different person.
8. ATTACK uses the existing town FIGHT/confirmation flow. No new combat system or automatic retaliation added.
9. WANTED retains its existing red styling, now outside the hidden early-level utility panel. Tim's SMALL TALK exposes PAY FINE, which preserves lifetime totals/history.
10. QA_MODE.html now loads crime.js in production script order. Previously it omitted the crime system entirely.
